Swim Endurance Challenge

Compete in speed and endurance races while refining your technique with professional swim coaches.

Register now

Register now

Start

Jun 1, 2025

Location:

Miami Aquatic Center, Miami, FL

Event details

Test your stamina, technique, and mental resilience in the Swimming Endurance Challenge, an open-water race designed for swimmers of all levels. Whether you're an experienced competitor or looking to push your limits in a long-distance swim, this event will challenge your endurance while giving you the chance to experience a breathtaking course in the open sea.

  • The race will take place in the warm, crystal-clear waters of the Gulf of Mexico.

  • Buoys and marker points will be placed throughout the course to guide swimmers.

What to expect

  • Professionally timed races and rankings.

  • On-site swim coaches offering stroke analysis and endurance tips.

  • Recovery stations with hydration, nutrition, and mobility guidance.

  • Special awards for top finishers and age group winners.

Who should attend?

  • Competitive swimmers looking to push their endurance.

  • Triathletes training for open-water racing.

  • Fitness enthusiasts seeking a new challenge.

  • Anyone passionate about swimming and adventure!
